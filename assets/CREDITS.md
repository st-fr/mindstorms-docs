# Poster assets

## Ferris

`ferris-happy.png` is the Happy Ferris artwork by Karen Rustad Tölva, obtained from https://rustacean.net/assets/rustacean-flat-happy.png.

Source and public-domain dedication: https://rustacean.net/.

## Universität Bayreuth

`universitaet-bayreuth.png` is the logo supplied by the user in Downloads as `Universitat_Bayreuth.svg.png`. It is placed with its original colors and proportions.

## EV3 robot illustrations

`ev3-game-style.png` was generated from the user-supplied photograph of the project robot.

`ev3-three-robots-transparent.png` was generated from that illustration for the poster header. It contains three EV3-style karts in motion on a transparent background. The generation prompt is recorded in `ev3-three-robots-prompt.md`.
